# EXP-011: degree-tight functions vs product trees on 4 variables

**Question.** Is every f with MC(f) = deg(f) - 1 ("tight") a product tree P*Q + affine (P, Q product trees whose degrees add up)?
**Result** (`pt4.py`, exact on 4 variables). The numbers of tight functions and of product trees coincide for k = 2, 3 and 4 (1,120; 30,720; 32,768).
**Status.** This is evidence only. The general conjecture is open, and the 5-variable test (planned as EXP-012) was not run in cycle 002.
