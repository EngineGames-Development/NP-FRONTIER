## Relevant theorem (abstract level)

For every quadratic Boolean function q(x_1, …, x_n), that is deg q = 2, the function x_1 x_2 ⋯ x_n ⊕ q(x_1, …, x_n) has multiplicative complexity **exactly n − 1**.

The lower bound is the degree bound. The content of the paper is the matching upper bound.

## Why it matters for NP-FRONTIER

- Natural explicit candidates of degree n do **not** beat n − 1: adding any quadratic to the full monomial keeps MC at exactly n − 1. This is relevant to index item (c).
- These are degree-tight functions, so the fresh-variable lemma (a) and the AND-direct sum (b) hold for them trivially via the degree bound.
